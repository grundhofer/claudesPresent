A natural counterpart to 004 and irresistible bilingually. *Keep it honest*: people without the word could still *see* blue — "the Greeks were colour-blind" is a myth. Needs the fact-check crew, which is exactly where language-comparison claims earn it: "German has no word for X" and "English had to borrow Y" were both overclaims until checked.
